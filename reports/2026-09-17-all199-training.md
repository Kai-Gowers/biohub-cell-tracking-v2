# 2026-09-17 -- Training on all 199 volumes for the final-submission weights

## Why

Leaderboard 0.927 (our weights, 179 training volumes) vs 0.942 for the reference weights, which were trained
with the same recipe on all 199 volumes. Locally the gap between our weights and theirs was entirely
detection recall (96.7 vs 98.3 %, `reports/2026-09-16-ddp-score-sweep.md`), and more training data is the
most direct lever on recall. Everything else in the pipeline is already identical.

## What changes

`scripts/train.py --train-on-all` (new; `TrainConfig.train_on_all`): the train set becomes all volumes,
while `val_score` is still computed on the usual 20-volume split. That validation is now **leaky** -- a
train-set number, useful as a smoothness / sanity signal, useless as a generalisation estimate. Checkpoints
record `train_on_all=True` and `scripts/score_local.py --held-out` prints a warning for them.

Checkpoint choice without clean validation: the reference picked epoch 381 of 400 by its own leaky
validation. Our clean runs plateaued from epoch ~120 (val_score 0.86-0.88, `_best` vs `_last` ~0.01, and
epoch 150 scored the same as epoch 385 through the full pipeline), so the plan is a **fixed late epoch**
(`_epoch375` / `_epoch400`; `--save-every 25`), with the leaky `_best` as a tie-break only.
